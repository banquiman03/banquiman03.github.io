Whether you prefer a traditional holiday play or you're looking for a funky, festive twist to the season, the Denver Center for the Performing Arts has you covered! Come in from the cold and reconnect with the joy of the season with two experiences the whole family will love.

Nov 16 - Dec 24
Back at Stanley Marketplace

"Camp Christmas turns the holiday spirit all the way up." - The Denver Post

Discover your holiday superpower, pay a visit to Santa, or savor craft cocktails as you wander through a 360-degree kaleidoscope of yuletide cheer. Scratch your head at playful pun trees, snap a pic in front of our nostalgic holiday displays, and most importantly, reconnect with the joy of the season. Camp Christmas is fun, fleeting, and just a little bit weird.

Nov 17 - Dec 24
Wolf Theatre

"The region's best production." - The Denver Post

Banish the "Bah, Humbug" spirit with Charles Dickens' timeless tale, A Christmas Carol. This heartwarming musical adaptation illuminates the meaning of the holiday season in a way that has resonated for generations.
