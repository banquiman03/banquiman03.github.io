About

I’m a Front-End Developer and Designer based in the Philippines. I enjoy working on both sides of a website—figuring out how it should look and feel, then turning that design into a responsive website that people can explore and interact with. Beyond how it looks and works, I also want every website to serve a clear purpose and support the goals behind it, whether that means bringing in more traffic, driving sales, generating inquiries, or helping customers book an appointment.

What I enjoy most is bringing a brand into the website itself. The colors, type, layout, and small details should feel like they belong to the same identity, not just something placed on a page. Seeing that come together as a working website is one of the most satisfying parts of the process for me.

I also enjoy working as part of a team. Collaborating with other developers, designers, and the people involved in a project gives me the chance to share ideas, hear different perspectives, and work together toward a better final result.

Thanks for taking the time to look around. I hope something here catches your eye—and gives you a good sense of the kind of work I enjoy creating.

What I'm doing

Web Design

Web Development

Mobile Apps Design

Creating static, dynamic, and responsive email campaigns that stay consistent with the brand and display properly across different email clients and devices.

How I Approach My Work

Discover

I start by understanding what the project needs to achieve, who it is for, and what users need from it.

Strategize

Once the goals are clear, I plan how the content, user journey, and key features should work together.

Design

I turn that direction into a visual experience that reflects the brand and feels natural to use.

Develop

I bring the approved design into the browser with responsive and maintainable code.

Refine

I improve the details that make the website feel better to use, from spacing to interactions and animations.

Validate

I test the website to make sure everything works as intended across devices and browsers.

Document

I keep important design and development details organized so the project is easier to maintain and build on.

Deploy

Once everything is ready, I prepare the website for launch and its live environment.
